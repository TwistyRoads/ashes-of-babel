# Story Origins

This directory preserves the prose story that served as the narrative source for **Ashes of Babel — The Fracture Point**.

## Source story

[`Ashes_of_Babel.md`](Ashes_of_Babel.md) contains the original prose version of the story.

The prose establishes the central narrative material later adapted into the interactive project, including Babel's destruction, Cain and Neba's examination of the event, the Anunna conflict surrounding it, and the fracture created by exploring what might have happened if a critical decision had changed.

## From prose to interactive narrative

The playable version is an adaptation rather than a direct transcription.

The interactive project restructures the source material around player choice, branching interpretation, persistent state, and multiple endings. Scenes, dialogue, sequencing, and decision points were revised or expanded where necessary to support interactivity while preserving the core characters, events, questions, and world established by the prose.

This makes the repository useful as both a playable narrative and an example of adaptation from linear fiction into an implemented branching system.
